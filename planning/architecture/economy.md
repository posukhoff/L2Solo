**Names mislead:** a `Cold*` module in Economy is usually a main-thread service both hot and cold call

`Population/ColdEconomyPlan.js`.

## Mechanisms

- **Wish engine:** `EconomyContext.forState` + `WishNetwork`/`WishProviders`/ `DecisionSections`/`Situations`. Runs in the worker. Main reads it via `ColdEconomyDecision.economyFor` (far bot → card, near bot → held decision). Main builds only `EconomyContext.basics`; the restocks (ShotStock, HealingPotionStock, ScrollStock, NpcRestockPlan, `ColdMarketService` funding) and the sale read the build's kit rates instead (`keptKit` / `keptTargets`, see the owners).
- **Worker market plan:** `ColdEconomyPlan.prepare/decide` → `{take, sell, withdraw, buyAds, shot, bidLimits}` (byte capped), from `MarketListingPolicy`, `BuyAdPolicy`, `MarketTownPolicy`, `ReadyTradeChoice`. Main runs it with `BotAfkMarketService.executePlan`. A sell row carries the width (`sigma`) of the belief its price was decided with (`ColdEconomyPlan.sellSigma`), so main builds no belief to publish it; buy rows still do (`lineState`, ~230 ms of 14 main minutes).
- **Main reconcile:** `BotAfkMarketService.reconcile` → `reconcileBuyAds`/`reconcileSellShop` recomputes lines on main; the sell shop only when its bag key moved (`stockSignature`: fired personal and beginner shots stay out while the published kit holds and no arrows are held). Only prices moved: `repriceSellLines`; lines moved: `publishSellShop`, which keeps a sell shop's record in its town (`Database` `republishSellShopUnsafe`: revision + 1, unchanged lines unwritten, a count moves only its difference) instead of closing and reopening it.
- **Cold town visit:** `ColdMarketListingService.open`: enchant → NPC cleanup → `saleDecision` → `ColdMarketBuyStoreService.sellToBestBuyer` → `StaticBuyerService.sell` → NPC liquidation → `depositCold` → `listOnBoard`. The board writer's "no" to a bot's sell listing (a unit held for its gear, craft or clan, `economy_material_protected`; a moved stack; a cap) is a refusal its shop and ad publishers return with the reason (`BotAfkMarketService.listingRefusal`, counted `market:listing_refused:<reason>`): the visit is deferred, never a command fault. A visit sells when it came to sell or holds a sale step (`visitSells`); a sale step past its visit is spent heading back (`EconomyContext.saleSpent`: done if its items left, refused if the kinds left are on the sale retry `marketSellSeen`, else held). A held improvement runs first in the step's work (`applyColdResolve`, the one cold site; one pass stay after it); henna/SA/soul crystal only where the NPC stands (`TownServiceCatalog.improvementTowns`), else `improvement:no_station` + goal_failed {impossible}. A visit's plan sells to no WTB line (`Ready.sale` only off a visit): answering a WTB is field trade, not built.
- **Own lines (`own_lines`):** worker decision far/near (`ColdSimulationWorker.ownLines` → `BoardLook.review` → `MarketPricing.look`); wakes `arrived_town`(:own), `line_undercut` (new SELL line past the line's noise: `MarketPricing.rivalMoved`), `goal_done:line` (`MarketBuyerWaiters.lineEvents`); look rides proposal/near `market`; unapplied → `relookOwnLines`. Hot bots never open shops/ads themselves.
- **Hot shopping:** `AI/States/ShoppingState` with `TradeService`, `AfkTradeService.buyFromShop/sellToShop`, `NpcRestockPlan.purchaseForActor`, `MarketOpportunity`.
- **Cold purchase:** `ColdMarketService.acquire` ("the one purchase path"; a visit's errand elsewhere departs at its end, `GoalExecutor.finishVisit`; a crafter buys inputs/recipe only where it stands (`ColdOccupationSources.preparePurchase`, `ColdShotEconomyService.recheck`; field: bag only; temporary: §7); errands, gear visits claim lines while their trip lasts (`BoardClaims`)); NPC baskets `NpcRestockPlan` → `NpcPurchaseBasket`. `BoardClaims`: first come wins, a player buys as ever; claims live in main's memory (`board_claims` worker table), a main restart drops them (accepted).
- **Board:** `AfkTrade/AfkTradeService` (records, projections, settlement), `BoardIndex` (shared with the worker), `TradeMeetingService` (field deals), `PlayerBoardService` (player UI).
- **Store transactions:** `Bot/TradeService` (configured merchants, NPC packets; live player stores via `PrivateStoreTrade`), `PrivateStore`, `BotTradeService` (player ↔ bot window), `BotNegotiationService`.
- **Board price chain:** `MarketPricing` → `PriceDecision` (one module for main and worker) → `PriceBelief` → `MarketCounters.firstPrice` → `FirstPrice`.
- **Offer search:** `OfferQuery` + `OfferOrder`, trip cost `OfferOrder.tripCost` → `EconomicTrip`; main through `MarketOpportunity.bestOffer`; one admission `OfferQuery.admits` (below).
- **Wallet:** `PurchaseFunding` ("one wallet rule for hot/cold purchases").
- **Required kit and survival stock:** a step of a funded required root (a `power:<item>:<slot>` kit piece, not a clan supply, or a `stock:` survival root; not an earning craft, crystal preparation or any other `progressionPriority` holder) is marked `required` by `WishNetwork` (kept in `Agenda` STEP_FIELDS). `EconomicValuation.roadCarried`, read by `visitPays` and `tripPays` (the build and its agenda, an errand at `acquire`), decides whether its road pays: already paid on a full fill; a part fill carries it iff share × benefit > (1 − share) × road; never a road dearer than the goods it brings (moneyPrice × fees + hours ≤ moneyPrice × cost). Leaf values stay the wish's benefit. `Agenda.nextSeller` lets a required step go up to its funded unit price, not beyond. A `stock:` step for an item no longer the kit's ends at its check (`EconomyContext.kitMoved`, `check:kit`: a shot step's kind is the build's, only the worn grade is checked). Required stock whose road its goods do not carry (at the bot's hour) holds no tier, as unsupplied stock (T178): the kit tier goes on, the stock rides along in the agenda of a visit or is bought in town. The road is weighed once, by the decision that takes the trip (the build's `visitPays`, an errand's `ColdMarketService.acquire` → `tripPays`); the held leaf's trip starts once per decision (hot `HuntingState`, `GoalExecutor.beginMarketTravel`: no second worth check, the market is read on arrival). An arrival on the leaf's own trip (`DecisionSections.leafVisit`; leaf: `ColdEconomyDecision.stepLeaf`) raises `arrived_town:own` (workshop only), that visit serves the card; others build. The visit, the trip and the tier read one step shape (`roadCarried`: unit price × units the seller fills, their share of the need). Our choice: a far bot with no potions keeps hunting without them until a town visit (a kit trip, a full-bag sale, a death's respawn), so a missing potion never costs a trip of its own.
- **Warehouse:** hot `BotWarehouseService.depositActorAtWarehouse` (each write rechecks the actor at the NPC in its transaction; a party companion's overweight errand, `AI/CompanionInventoryCleanup` → `ShoppingState`, passes `unload`: free stock past the candidates, heaviest first, until below the first weight penalty; its arrows go to the NPC through `SellJunk`), cold `depositCold`, worker mirror `PersonalWarehouseIndex`, craft staging `CraftWarehousePolicy`.
- **Crafting for trade:** decisions in the worker (`WealthCraftDecision`, `ShotCraftPolicy`, `CraftProfitPolicy`, `ProductionSalePolicy`); execution on main (`ColdWealthCraftService`, `ColdShotEconomyService`, `HotWealthCraftService`, `ColdCraftingService`); `CraftShopService`, `CraftWorkshopService` (main's public index of the listings, w: rows). A smith's shot series is a financed half-hour demand window (`ShotProductionHorizon`: `HOURS`, `retainedBatches` kept from sale by `ItemDisposition.productionInputAmounts`; funding `ShotProductionFunding`; shot recipes bypass the generic intent path, DV-2); the gear a shot kit crystallizes is held by one owner, `ItemDisposition.kitGear` (30 min, limits.md "Shot craft series"): not sold, one unworn unenchanted copy kept out of `GearAcquisitionPlanner.equipInventoryUpgrades`, a worn copy stays worn (`BotCrystallization` buys it beside a worn copy). A far crafter's craft step (`plan.shot`) runs in `BotAfkMarketService.executePlan` only (post-commit, or a visit's showcase; a throw counted `economy.visit_craft`). Value: product exit less inputs' `exitValue` (both at the floor: raw mats earn ~0)
- **Hour value and spot income:** `BotHuntEfficiency.huntIncome`/`huntHour` (the bot's own hunt samples, else the precomputed spot table through `SpotValueTable`), `SpotEconomics.create` (crowding, calibration) — what every trip, purchase and wish prices an hour with; shots / potions pay (`ShotStock.usePolicy`, `EconomyContext.foundation`). Money of a kill is live (`SpotRewardExpectation.value`). See [tables.md](tables.md).
- **Decision links' counts** (developerDiagnostics only): `link:*` and the worker's `links=` status line: the route rows as a shadow of a key on `DecisionSections.LINK_INPUTS`. The earning spot's searches and their time are on the `situations=` line (`search:<n>/<ms>ms`, `EquipmentIncomeRoute.searchTotals`, diagnostics off too).
- **Observability:** `EconomyDiagnostics` (kept source facts: `projection:fact:<kept|moved:<input>|absent>`, one in 16 verified against a fresh walk, `projection:verify`; kept gear candidates: `projection:gear:<kept|absent|unkeyed|moved:<input>|<read>>`, `projection:gear_unkept:<size|shape>`, one in 16 weighed fresh too, `projection:gear_verify`), `MarketTelemetry`, `CraftTelemetry`, `MarketSnapshot`, `src/EconomyJournal`, `src/MarketTradeOverview*`, `src/MarketEconomyOverview`.

## Data flow

**Cold:** a situation that wakes the build (`Situations.DECISIONS`, population.md "Situations") → worker `EconomyContext.forState` → `ColdEconomyPlan.prepare` → main `executePlan` writes board records. Field deal:
`ReadyTradeChoice` → `TradeMeetingService` → `AfkTradeService.finalizeTrade`. Physical buys: `GoalExecutor.beginMarketTravel`
sends the bot; in town `PopulationService` → `ColdMarketService.tryPurchase` (goal) or `acquire` (craft inputs, clan,
raid supply) → board / NPC / configured merchant (`buyNpcStack`, direct `Database.purchaseNpcInventoryItem`).

**Hot:** goal from the held decision → `ShoppingState` target order: workshop, equipment stop, live buyer (board vs
merchant bots), warehouse, buyer again, NPC sell-junk. Shots/potions/scrolls through `ShotStock`, `HealingPotionStock`,
`ScrollStock` with their own offer lists.
