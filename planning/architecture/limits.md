Caps, budgets, size bounds, rates, top-K cuts and what happens at each as the world grows. ## Top risks (silent when hit, likely at ~1700 bots)
1. Governor: DB queue 8 with a player defers the safety sweep, cold snapshot continuation pages, full-window commits, clan actions/founders, hot market review (dD only).
2. State retention and WAL truncate share the governor's 50 ms/s and DB queue 8 with the player: under a steady write queue they defer (counted) and WAL grows past 256 MB until a quiet second, the 10-min retry or the 512 MB force (a pause over 50 ms).
3. Rival window: busy items get no forecast, new asks at NPC buyback, no counter.
4. Activation pool: duplicates filtered after the 100-nearest cut leave crowded towns few or no activations near the player, unrecorded (travellers are shown by `showWalkersNear` instead).
5. Scan windows (top-5 buy exits): own/rejected/custody lines use the window, ready trades quietly fail.
6. Cold buffs: past 6 sales a minute world-wide `sale_limit` is discarded by Coordinator (dD total only).
7. Wish route requests: past 64 in flight [], reviews run without trip data in catch-up bursts.
8. Hot event queue: drops above 1024 pending, drains ~64/s: social memory lost in crowded fights.
9. Path pool: under a busy pool NPC chases lose first; mobs without line of sight stand and keep hating until the 2 min attack timeout, as in C4 (dD).
10. Sockets (Session ~222, ~231): no write backpressure (no `writableLength`/drain check in src), no broadcast fan-out cap (real players within 6000): a slow client's write buffer grows without bound in a dense zone.
11. Sale candidates: cheap stacks never sold, bags fill.
12. `EconomyContext` retain: above ~2000 bots or > 64 hot bots, full rebuild every time (dD).
13. Warehouse index: long-lived bots lose sight of the rest of their stock.
14. Incremental snapshot row: one over 256 KB stalls every hot→cold snapshot queued behind it until that state shrinks (dD `send_failed`).
Next in line (rows): InteractionMemory, Group pair events (9-member raids), Clan goals, ClanPartyHelp (refused silently), Spot room clamp 9-36 (sends bots off-band), Proposal payload (requeued every 5 s forever), State page (10 s retry never read). Not silent but binding: Steps in flight with a player online (status `coldDue`).
