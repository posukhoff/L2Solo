## Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Decides | Entry |
|---|---|---|
| `BotAI.tick` | Order of guards per hot tick: PvP defense, board meeting, clan-alliance AI, trade, buff duty, LOD gate, chat arrival, the rebuff's buffer purchase (`BuffService.buyOnAnswer`, once per answer), death, loot, clan hall, hot competition, hot bot-party, then `States[session.plan]` (a state that throws is held off and leaves its plan at the 5th fault in a row: `stateFaulted`, `BotFaultHold`) | `BotAI.scheduleTick` via `HotAiDispatcher`; the 5th fault in a row also raises `goal_failed {impossible}`: the held leaf is re-decided, never retried |
| `AI/States/*` | What the body does: hunting, following, resting, shopping, getting_buffed, fleeing, pk_* | `state.tick(...)` from `BotAI.tick` |
| Activity / occupation | Which job (hunting, selling, shopping, crafting, improving). **Decided by the worker**; a hot body only reads it | `DecisionEvents.held(session).network.activity` |
| Re-decision | When the held decision is stale (woken decisions: [population.md](population.md)) | `DecisionEvents.raiseDecision(session, situation)` (a name of the catalogue: `arrived_town`, `revived`, `goal_failed`, `party_left`, `spot_empty`, `money`); near raise goes to the worker. Main's executors raise their outcome on the row (`DecisionEvents.raiseOn`: `goal_done:goal` a purchase landed, `goal_failed` a refused purchase or meeting; a refused review raises nothing); a body's failed gear errand waits for the answer to its `goal_failed` (`DecisionEvents.failureWaits`, no retry window). The step's own facts: `Situations.detect(before, after, round)` (one diff both threads, names only; `factNames` also read by the held build's verdict). Whether a far bot hears it now: `Bot/Situations.js` (T189: only at its player's moment; our rule). A body's `arrived_town` is raised by `ShoppingState` at visit stage ends (≥ 9 sites, several per visit), never at the arrival (town-10) |
| Solo spot step | Go to a spot or search locally | `BotDecisionService.suggest` (from HuntingState only) |
| Hot target | Which mob | `BotTargetScorer`, `BotHuntingTargetPolicy`, `BotHuntingGroundPolicy`; solo: ready fights first (`HuntingState.readyRanked`), line of sight in bounded batches that resume where a blocked one stopped, over every candidate before the scan gives up (`BotHuntingVisibility.batch` / `select`, limits.md); the planned mob (`PartyHuntingTarget.soloNpcId`: gear plan's next hunt, else the hunting leaf's mob; hot and cold) is a preference among the spot's eligible unclaimed mobs, never a filter; an empty scan counts `target_scan:empty:<gate>` once per streak; party: `Population/PartyHuntingTarget` (hot and cold) |
| Rest | Sit or not | `AI/RestPolicy` (hot, cold, BotStatus) |
| Far-solo goals | A far bot's goal, computed where read (no copy, no review clock) | `Goals/NeedsEvaluator.goal`/`needs` (floor, pending errand, else the held card's leaf, amount = `remainingToOrder`; main and worker, null when hot) → `GoalExecutor` (the trip); clan line at a moved card (`ColdEconomyDecisions` accept/hold → `BotClanChat.onDecision`); observers (status windows, clan chat flush, `WorldObserver`) read `NeedsEvaluator.shownGoal`: the held card's goal, current or stale; a throw shows none (`needs.shown_goal`) |
| Hot bot-only party | Leader, spot return, buffs, raid prep; unlike cold it dissolves a material party on unsafe ground (`HotPartyLifecycle.releaseUnsafeHunt`, parties-6) and revives by `PartyRevivalService` (60 s wait, town, walk back; parties-7 in combat-1) | `AI/HotBackgroundParty` |
| Player's companions | Follow, assist, heal, buff, rest | `States/FollowingState` + `PartyCompanionService` |
| Party admission | Whether a bot joins the player | `AI/BotAvailability.evaluate` (session) / `evaluateState` (cold) |
| Player chat → action | LLM on: `BotDialogueArbiter` → `BotBrain` → `BotAgentTools`. LLM off (default): regex `BotManager.applyPlayerSpeechRules` | `BotManager.handlePlayerSpeak` |
| Ambient chat | Bot scenes and chatter, under `BotChatterBudget` | `BotManager.checkAndStartConversation` (`BotAmbientDirector`) |

Rule stated in code (`BotAI.js` ~527, enforced by `ALLOWED_EVENTS` in `BotBrain.js` ~22): autonomous state changes belong to the
deterministic brain; the LLM acts only on explicit player communication.
