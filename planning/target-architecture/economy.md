## Leanings (maintainer; DM1-DM25 2026-11-07, later ones dated; all open)

- DM3 rare set at x1-x5 (recipes, key parts, enchant scrolls, SA crystals, raid jewels); commons at the server rate.
- DM1 fixed buy stores removed for bots.
- DM5 mild inflation is normal (faucets grow with play time and level, sinks optional, no adena lost on death in C4; retail L2 inflated for years); harmful when money per character outgrows its band's goods (drop-only prices run away). Target: money per bot stationary within its band, aggregate grows only with the level mix; % sinks, never a clock.
- DM9 castle tax: the C4 rule (R13; Q1).
- DM13 regional trade chat (C4 `TradeChat`) replaces the global WTS/WTB ads; a bot knows what a player there could know.
- DM14 prices differ by town; Giran the hub, Dion also important, other towns to be worked out; stores gravitate to the towns where bots gather most.
- DM15 the world closes gaps itself: bot traders seek margins; bot errors rare.
- DM16 bots enchant past safe by character (a sink).
- DM17 rich bots compete with the player; top gear is mostly the clan's (a clan dresses its leader/core; a player in a clan gets dressed by it).
- DM18 bargaining real with the player; between cold bots a seeded roll from both characters adjusts the deal price.
- DM19 entry: the world exactly as left (no catch-up).
- DM20 a store is cold light data, not a simulated actor; an actor only in the player's view; view range per the client, we may stream/cap near the player.
- DM21 trader alt windows (most C4 players traded through one): an account runs several characters at once (our choice; C4: one online per account); no account grouping (account = the player, characters = windows), each hunting or sitting in a store; future twin accounts/sessions/rotation REPLACE this seam, never a parallel mechanism.
- DM22 goods reach the alt via the free shared account warehouse.
- DM23 second windows only for the market for now. DM24 manufacture stores in scope.
- DM8 crafters keep AFK buy stores for inputs; hunters sell mats to them before NPCs.
- DM25 store count follows the bots (own supply, needs, characters), not what the player should see.
- DM6 the player most likely never sits in a store, but may put up an AFK store (away or in another window, while the server runs); bots buy from it (same data as a bot's sitting window); his other sell paths: bots' stores/WTB, trade chat, NPC.
- Stores 2026-11-08: trader alts only for trader/crafter personas plus accounts holding goods worth > 1 h of hunting (G2); manufacture stores not touched now, the stationary crafters exist (G4, `bot_craft_*` untouched); a crafter's buy store sits on its trader alt (Q4).
- DM12 crafters/players know the world's stage and their own path (R15).
- Rates 2026-11-08: the product offers presets x1, x10, x50 (`ProgressionRates.PRESETS`; the launcher also exposes expRate/spRate/adenaRate/dropChanceRate/spoilRate); the economy stays healthy at all three; measure G and s (§Rates b) at each, x1 the C4 reference. Leaning: one multiplier per preset for exp, sp, adena, drop, spoil (keeps C4 ratios: G as at x1); rarity by the drop rule (a4: rate scales counts/rolls, weights inside a group x1); epics x1 and the rare set ~x1-x5 through that rule, not new rates; a consumable cost knob per preset by measurement; launcher rates stay user overrides, presets do not split them. Risk: adena surplus at x50 → sinks (tax, enchanting) first; adena skew below exp only as fallback.
- Price errors 2026-11-08: a misjudged value, not a typo: from limited knowledge (item far above its grade, outside its class/role, never seen traded, a rare drop), shrinks with experience; traders and crafters judge their own goods well; no fixed error %; the player and bot traders profit where knowledge is thin (formula §Directions).
- Population 2026-11-08: a fresh-server cohort (bots start low, starterBotsPerRace 30, progress with the world; 1700 = concurrent cap `maxPlayingPopulation`); the player's market depends on his place vs the cohort (inside: full; ahead: raids/craft/rare; behind: cheap outgrown gear): not a gap; R15 follows the cohort. A fresh world starts with empty stores, no seed (G9 dropped).
- Item no NPC sells (ok 2026-11-08): floor npcBuy = ref/2; seller start = known price, else craft cost (also the ceiling), else worth of same-grade/slot comparables, ref last; steps down per unsold look; buyers bid by worth, never by ref, so a ref out of line with use (a weaker weapon dearer) is corrected by deals; rare non-craftables: buyer competition.
- Clans 2026-11-08: Q-C1..C7 answered: money in by clan type (competitive obligations, casual voluntary); dress order by type, the player sets his; levels by the leader at C4 x1; halls later, castles NPC; the player is the leader (convention); founding C4 lv10, count emergent (no share target); LLM for chat lines only. Clan lv3 reachable with simplified requirements; war declaration decided after situations step 9.
- Clans 2026-11-08: the player may lead a clan he did not create; clan decisions (dress order, spending, donations, WH access, raids, wars) read one settings record written by its leader (bot or player); temporary: the player becomes leader automatically on joining. A raid clan is also a PvP clan; competitive clans (top raids, spots, castles, halls, PvP for it) impose obligations, casual ask nothing. The player in a clan is a separate topic (the world is for him): no obligations bind him (what he gives is noticed: chat, queue priority); he sets competitiveness, dress queue, bots' dues, raids, wars, with detailed settings and full management; soft consequences only (chat); the clan serves him (crafter at cost, buffers, raids on his call).
- Deferred (no step now): Seven Signs/ancient adena; sessions; rotation; DM11 circulation (entry/retirement): must not break when it comes (prices per item, not per bot; one close path for a bot's stores/escrow/claims/meetings); spoiler/crafter and buffer windows; DM7 the wish engine may be redesigned.
- Field trade both ways (decided 2026-11-07): a hunting bot answers a WTB for what it holds and a WTS for what it needs. Open: "worth the walk?"; C4 replies at once (= claim) and meets when convenient: the keener side (often the author) comes to the holder's spot, or at its next town visit; no leaving a party; a hunt breaks off at once only for a rare/expensive item, when it ends anyway (potions out, bag full), or for a real player; other deals wait. Built with the redesigned market.
- Contention as trade (maintainer 2026-11-07): bots wanting one line bid; the claim (`BoardClaims`, first come today) goes to the highest payer and the price rises.
