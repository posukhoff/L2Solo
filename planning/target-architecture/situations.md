# Situations: maintainer answers still binding, open questions
The mechanism as built (store, places, DECISIONS, raisers, aggregates, trace, registry): architecture/population.md "Situations". This file keeps only the maintainer's answers that bind future changes and what is not decided.
## Maintainer answers (binding)
- Q1 town wakes purchases only, the spot after shopping is held. Q2 `spot_disappoints` at a rest, once a stay, after N kills, own income under the held expectation beyond its error. Q3 the spot is an aggregate: an arrival over room raises `spot_contested`, decided once: who holds it by visible look, the stronger displacing the weaker, an even arrival leaving, friends sharing < 2× room, no grievance from a displacement, standing parties not displaced yet (11-09 F-C1..F-C7); standing bots are not woken by the count; no crowd scan or replay. Q4 money wakes only `goal_affordable`/`out_of_money`. Q5 push only: the whole market on board events with per-item waits, party and clan wants too (trade chat). Q6 no field ask on loot; field trade only on `buyer_posted` or a meeting request. Q7 a level is no situation; what it opens (`grade_changed`, `spot_outleveled`) is heard at the next stop.
- Q8 one rule hot and cold; player-facing situations immediate. Q9 deadlines set once when a wait starts, each a limits.md row. Q10 a decision's new answer wakes no dependants. Q11 diff for own state, explicit raises by owners for world facts; only `raise` writes bits. Q12 `gear_changed` heard in town only; field equip upgrades stay a system. Q13 relations wake nothing; read at forming, contest, PvP/revenge. Q14 goals are a view over the card (`NeedsEvaluator.goal`), no goal copy. Q15 the clan raises to bots, parties and the board; its planners' windows go with the clan planners plan. A party has an objective.
- N1 one owner of the spot aggregate, the worker; main posts hot arrivals/leaves, its index a mirror. N2 `spot_disappoints` compares with the held answer's expected income. N3 a party hears only what changes it whole (member joined/left, contest, danger, empty, outleveled spot); a member's death or band shift wakes only that member (`party_stay`, `party_outleveled`). N4 a party's stop is its round end; wipe, leave, contest, clan call immediate. N5 strategic party decisions on the worker, hot and cold; tactics on main.
- A1 hot detect on main over the posted row. A2 `enemy_met` raised by the spot when a bot and a grievance peer (killer, clan enemy) meet, either arriving; the shared karma-aware PvP/revenge rule decides. A3 an assembling party ends on its members' known ends (arrival, readiness, leaving the objective), no assembly deadline unless one is missed. A4 forming stays on main until a perf run shows it on main's profile; main commits the roster with the membership transaction.
- A5 potions are not mandatory; rest drinks stay economic (`RestPotion`); `stock_low:potions` wakes earning spot and build: stay, buy, or move to a spot needing none. "Needs drinks" = the row's measured potions at the bot's gap > 0, adjusted for gear by taken × pull × `DRINK_TAIL` 1.5 vs maxHp × (1 − useThreshold) (ours); `died`/`spot_dangerous` the fallback; a no-potion table only if deaths demand it.
- A6 `buffs_expired` (20-min buffs, `died` clears) wakes the own decision `rebuff` (none, Newbie Guide, clan hall, a buffer at the spot): field trips only within reach, no pricing; Guide cap 25 owned by `BotBuffs` (ours); being a buffer for hire is an open economy leaf (DT-74).
- B2 Scroll of Escape not mandatory: one per outing when its saved hours beat its price, none in towns karma closes. B3 paused: Seven Signs and day/night as world situations. B4 a weapon bridge is a party-leave reason only if funded; a funded bridge is bought via a build survival wish `bridge:weapon`, woken by `gear_changed`/`party_left`/`goal_affordable:focus`. B5 `party_stay` wakes on `goal_affordable:focus`. B6 while karma > 0 the earning spot takes the karma-wash choice, woken by `karma_changed`; `ColdKarmaPolicy` reads the held answer. B7 `goal_affordable:potions` wakes earning spot and build.
- B8 `enemy_met` raisers: the spot (bot↔bot) and `CharacterVisibility.afterSend` (player↔bot, grievances both ways; roll keyed bot, player, the bot's stay sequence). B9 over room = per-target pressure at the arrival; whole-spot room stays the search's filter. B10 mixed hot/cold pairs counted (`enemy_met:mixed`), traced, no fight. B11 cold clan-war meetings go through `enemy_met`; measure, tune the weight. B12 the held earning answer is every solo bot's hunting spot; the leveling walk is a candidate inside the search. B13 training is no held decision: the level-up fact's consumer in the round; skills at level up anywhere are ours, not C4. B14 a unit on a player's command never gets `avoid` (`spot:contest:commanded`).
- B15 `bag_full` answers only "leave the field now?" (physical facts: slots, weight, counts); what to sell, list, deposit or give, and to whom, is the visit's decision at `arrived_town` (prices and board read then); a party member's `bag_full` wakes `party_stay`. One question per decision; a situation never computes another decision's answer.
- B16 a level is no situation: `grade_changed` when `GearGradeRule.gradeForLevel` moves either way (STOP → gear_plan, build, earning_spot); `spot_outleveled` when the held spot falls past `ProgressionRates.DEEP_BLUE_LEVEL_GAP` below the bot (C4 deep-blue), STOP → earning_spot. Not situations (rule 0): `class_change` (grade_changed covers it), a workshop level fact.
- B17 a far bot's town visit is its own unless its party called it (`ownMove`); not wakes: died → gear_plan/build, revived → build, party_left → gear_plan, stock_low:potions → earning_spot (A5 fallback); kept: player_command → build (the leaf names the spot), goal_done:visit → build (the agenda moves only in a woken build).
- C4 notes (ours, said in code): `offer_party` ≤ 5, level gap ≤ 4; raid respawn fixed 5 h (`RespawnPolicy`), `boss_spawned` a deadline. Player-led companions (errands, `CompanionInventoryCleanup`, alliance quest) stay.
